# Encounter Budget
When designing encounters using https://builder.pf2easy.com/ can help plan the exp budget and help determine the difficulty for warning players. Difficulty is a function of Party Size, level, and budget. If budget remains the same, going down in party size will increase difficulty by 1 step for each player less. The opposite is also true.

# Design
- **EXP Budget**: 60exp
- **Recommended Player Count**: 3-4
	- 3p = Moderate Encounter
	- 4p = Low Encounter
